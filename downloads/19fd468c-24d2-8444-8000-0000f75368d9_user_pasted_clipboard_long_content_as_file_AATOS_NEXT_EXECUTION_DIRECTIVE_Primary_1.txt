AATOS NEXT EXECUTION DIRECTIVE

Primary Objective

Populate and verify the Kenya to United States green specialty coffee compliance rule set, while the legal documents move through counsel review.

Do not expand to new corridors or commodities.

Do not build trade finance, insurance, mobile, AI, or enterprise features during this cycle.

Execution Scope

Origin: Kenya

Destination: United States

Commodity: Green, unroasted specialty coffee

Primary users:

Kenyan exporter or cooperative

U.S. importer

Compliance reviewer

Platform administrator

Required Outcomes

1. A verified compliance source register
2. A machine-readable rule set
3. A user-facing compliance checklist
4. A document request workflow
5. A reviewer approval workflow
6. Expiration tracking
7. Clear responsibility assignment
8. Manual fallback procedures
9. Audit evidence
10. Automated tests

## 2. Verified Compliance Rules

### 2.1 Kenyan Export Requirements

#### KE-001: Phytosanitary Certificate
- **Type:** certification
- **Requirement:** Obtain Phytosanitary Certificate from KEPHIS
- **Description:** Required for all green coffee exports. KEPHIS inspector examines consignment at production location or export packhouse. Certificate confirms freedom from quarantine pests and compliance with importing country requirements.
- **Responsible Party:** Exporter (Kenyan supplier)
- **Issuing Authority:** KEPHIS
- **Estimated Time:** 3-5 business days
- **Estimated Cost:** USD 50-150 per certificate
- **Validity:** Single shipment
- **Verification Method:** Visual inspection of original certificate; KEPHIS verification via reference number
- **Source:** https://kephis.org/services/phytosanitary-services/
- **Source Verified:** 2026-08-06

#### KE-002: Coffee Export License
- **Type:** permit
- **Requirement:** Valid Coffee Export License from AFA Coffee Directorate
- **Description:** All coffee exporters must be licensed by the Agriculture and Food Authority. License confirms registration and compliance with coffee industry regulations.
- **Responsible Party:** Exporter
- **Issuing Authority:** AFA — Coffee Directorate
- **Estimated Time:** 14-30 days (initial); annual renewal
- **Estimated Cost:** USD 200-500 annually
- **Validity:** 1 year
- **Verification Method:** License number verification with AFA
- **Source:** http://afa.go.ke/coffee-directorate/
- **Source Verified:** 2026-08-06

#### KE-003: Certificate of Origin
- **Type:** document
- **Requirement:** Preferential Certificate of Origin (for AGOA benefits)
- **Description:** Required to claim duty-free entry under African Growth and Opportunity Act (AGOA). Must be issued by designated authority and accompany shipment.
- **Responsible Party:** Exporter
- **Issuing Authority:** Kenya Revenue Authority or designated chamber
- **Estimated Time:** 1-2 business days
- **Estimated Cost:** USD 25-50
- **Validity:** Single shipment
- **Verification Method:** AGOA eligibility verification via USTR website
- **Source:** https://agoa.info/about-agoa.html
- **Source Verified:** 2026-08-06

#### KE-004: Export Declaration
- **Type:** document
- **Requirement:** Kenya Revenue Authority export declaration
- **Description:** Mandatory customs export documentation. Includes commercial invoice, packing list, and export permit details.
- **Responsible Party:** Exporter
- **Issuing Authority:** KRA
- **Estimated Time:** 1-2 business days
- **Estimated Cost:** USD 25-75 processing fee
- **Validity:** Single shipment
- **Verification Method:** KRA system reference
- **Source:** https://kra.go.ke/business/exporting-goods
- **Source Verified:** 2026-08-06

#### KE-005: Quality Inspection / Grading Certificate
- **Type:** inspection
- **Requirement:** Coffee quality grading and inspection
- **Description:** Green coffee must be graded according to Kenyan standards (AA, AB, PB, etc.) and may require quality certification depending on buyer requirements.
- **Responsible Party:** Exporter
- **Issuing Authority:** AFA Coffee Directorate or licensed cupper
- **Estimated Time:** 2-3 days
- **Estimated Cost:** USD 100-300 per lot
- **Validity:** Single lot/shipment
- **Verification Method:** Grading certificate review
- **Source:** http://afa.go.ke/coffee-directorate/
- **Source Verified:** 2026-08-06

### 2.2 United States Import Requirements

#### US-001: Prior Notice (FDA)
- **Type:** registration
- **Requirement:** FDA Prior Notice submission before shipment arrival
- **Description:** FDA requires advance notification of all food imports. Must be submitted electronically through FDA Prior Notice System Interface (PNSI) or Customs ABI system. Must include manufacturer, shipper, product details, anticipated arrival information.
- **Responsible Party:** Importer (U.S. buyer) or U.S. agent
- **Issuing Authority:** FDA
- **Estimated Time:** Same day (online submission)
- **Estimated Cost:** No fee if submitted directly; broker fees USD 25-75 if using customs broker
- **Deadline:** Must be submitted before arrival at port
- **Verification Method:** FDA PNSI confirmation number
- **Source:** https://www.fda.gov/food/importing-food-products-united-states/prior-notice-imported-foods-system
- **Source Verified:** 2026-08-06
- **Note:** Green coffee is a "food" subject to Prior Notice. Roasted coffee is also subject but may have different requirements.

#### US-002: Facility Registration (FDA)
- **Type:** registration
- **Requirement:** Foreign Supplier Verification Program (FSVP) compliance
- **Description:** Under FSMA, U.S. importers must verify their foreign suppliers are producing food in compliance with U.S. standards. Green coffee importers need FSVP program documenting hazard analysis and verification activities.
- **Responsible Party:** Importer
- **Issuing Authority:** FDA
- **Estimated Time:** Ongoing program (not per-shipment)
- **Estimated Cost:** USD 2,000-10,000 annually (program maintenance, third-party audits if needed)
- **Validity:** Ongoing; annual renewal of food facility registration
- **Verification Method:** FSVP documentation review; FDA inspection
- **Source:** https://www.fda.gov/food/importing-food-products-united-states/foreign-supplier-verification-programs-fsma
- **Source Verified:** 2026-08-06

#### US-003: Customs Entry (CBP)
- **Type:** document
- **Requirement:** CBP customs entry with HTS classification
- **Description:** All imports require customs entry. Green coffee HTS codes:
  - 0901.11.00 — Coffee, not roasted, not decaffeinated (arabica)
  - 0901.12.00 — Coffee, not roasted, decaffeinated
  - 0901.21.00 — Coffee, roasted, not decaffeinated
  - 0901.22.00 — Coffee, roasted, decaffeinated
- **Responsible Party:** Importer or licensed customs broker
- **Issuing Authority:** CBP
- **Estimated Time:** 1-5 business days (depending on examination)
- **Estimated Cost:** Broker fees USD 100-300; MPF fees ~0.3464% of value (min USD 31.67, max USD 614.35)
- **Validity:** Single entry
- **Verification Method:** CBP entry number
- **Source:** https://www.cbp.gov/trade/professional-entry
- **Source Verified:** 2026-08-06

#### US-004: Tariff and Duty
- **Type:** tax
- **Requirement:** Duty assessment under HTS 0901.11.00
- **Description:** Green coffee (unroasted) enters the U.S. duty-free under AGOA. Must have valid Certificate of Origin to claim preference. Without AGOA certificate, standard duty applies (varies; check current rates).
- **Responsible Party:** Importer
- **Issuing Authority:** CBP
- **Estimated Time:** At time of entry
- **Estimated Cost:** USD 0 with AGOA certificate; otherwise standard duty rates
- **Verification Method:** CBP duty determination
- **Source:** https://hts.usitc.gov/
- **Source Verified:** 2026-08-06

#### US-005: USDA Phytosanitary Import Requirements
- **Type:** inspection
- **Requirement:** APHIS phytosanitary inspection at port of entry
- **Description:** Green coffee beans are subject to USDA APHIS inspection. Must have valid Kenyan phytosanitary certificate. Coffee beans must be free from soil, plant debris, and quarantine pests. May be subject to inspection, sampling, or treatment.
- **Responsible Party:** Importer
- **Issuing Authority:** USDA APHIS
- **Estimated Time:** 1-3 days (if examination required)
- **Estimated Cost:** No direct fee; possible detention/storage costs
- **Validity:** Per shipment
- **Verification Method:** APHIS inspection report
- **Source:** https://www.aphis.usda.gov/aphis/ourfocus/planthealth/import-information
- **Source Verified:** 2026-08-06

#### US-006: FDA Inspection and Sampling
- **Type:** inspection
- **Requirement:** Possible FDA inspection and sampling
- **Description:** FDA may inspect, sample, and test imported coffee for pesticide residues, mycotoxins (ochratoxin A, aflatoxin), heavy metals, and filth. Green coffee is generally lower risk than roasted but not exempt.
- **Responsible Party:** Importer
- **Issuing Authority:** FDA
- **Estimated Time:** 2-10 business days (if sampled)
- **Estimated Cost:** Possible laboratory analysis fees if private lab used; otherwise no direct FDA fee
- **Validity:** Per shipment (if sampled)
- **Verification Method:** FDA notice of action
- **Source:** https://www.fda.gov/food/importing-food-products-united-states/imported-food-safety
- **Source Verified:** 2026-08-06

#### US-007: Importer of Record Number
- **Type:** registration
- **Requirement:** CBP importer of record identification
- **Description:** Importer must have valid IRS tax ID (EIN) and be registered with CBP. Must use standard entry procedures.
- **Responsible Party:** Importer
- **Issuing Authority:** IRS / CBP
- **Estimated Time:** Ongoing
- **Estimated Cost:** None
- **Verification Method:** CBP account
- **Source:** https://www.cbp.gov/trade/professional-entry
- **Source Verified:** 2026-08-06

#### US-008: State-Level Requirements
- **Type:** permit
- **Requirement:** State agricultural/quarantine permits (if applicable)
- **Description:** Some states (e.g., California, Hawaii) have additional agricultural import requirements. Coffee generally exempt but verify destination state regulations.
- **Responsible Party:** Importer
- **Issuing Authority:** State Department of Agriculture
- **Estimated Time:** Varies
- **Estimated Cost:** Varies
- **Verification Method:** State permit documentation
- **Source:** Varies by state
- **Source Verified:** 2026-08-06 (general framework; specific states need case-by-case verification)

## 3. Commercial and Documentation Requirements

#### COM-001: Commercial Invoice
- **Type:** document
- **Requirement:** Detailed commercial invoice
- **Description:** Must include seller/buyer details, product description (scientific name: Coffea arabica), quantity (kg), unit price, total value, Incoterms, payment terms, country of origin.
- **Responsible Party:** Exporter
- **Verification Method:** Document review

#### COM-002: Packing List
- **Type:** document
- **Requirement:** Detailed packing list
- **Description:** Must match commercial invoice. Include number of bags, gross/net weight, bag markings, lot numbers.
- **Responsible Party:** Exporter
- **Verification Method:** Document review

#### COM-003: Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
- **Type:** document
- **Requirement:** Transport document
- **Description:** Ocean bill of lading or air waybill evidencing contract of carriage. Must be clean and match other documents.
- **Responsible Party:** Carrier / Freight forwarder
- **Verification Method:** Document review

#### COM-004: Insurance Certificate
- **Type:** document
- **Requirement:** Marine cargo insurance (if CIF/DDP terms)
- **Description:** Evidence of insurance coverage for transit. Typically 110% of CIF value. Institute Cargo Clauses (A) recommended.
- **Responsible Party:** Exporter (if CIF) or Importer (if FOB)
- **Verification Method:** Certificate review

#### COM-005: Certificate of Analysis
- **Type:** laboratory_test
- **Requirement:** Quality and safety analysis (buyer-dependent)
- **Description:** May include moisture content, defect count, cupping score, pesticide residue analysis, mycotoxin screening. Required by some specialty buyers.
- **Responsible Party:** Exporter or third-party lab
- **Estimated Cost:** USD 200-1,000 per analysis
- **Verification Method:** Laboratory report review

## 5. Discrepancies and Caveats

### 5.1 Identified Gaps

1. **State-level requirements:** US-008 is framework-only. Specific states (California, Washington, etc.) may have additional requirements not fully catalogued here. Each shipment destination state should be verified individually.

2. **Organic certification:** If coffee is certified organic, additional USDA NOP requirements apply. Not included in baseline rule set.

3. **Fair Trade / Direct Trade:** Voluntary certifications not included. These are commercial, not regulatory.

4. **Kenya-specific local permits:** Single Business Permit, NEMA environmental compliance, etc. These are exporter-internal and not shipment-specific. Documented but not in critical path.

5. **Tariff rate changes:** HTS rates and AGOA eligibility are subject to policy changes. Rule set should be reviewed quarterly.

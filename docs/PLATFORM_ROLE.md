# AATOS Platform Role Definition

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Status:** Approved for pilot
**Authority:** AATOS 4-Week Trust and Pilot Readiness Directive

---

## What AATOS Is

AATOS is a **technology platform** that provides:

1. **Marketplace operator** — Connects verified agricultural suppliers with verified buyers
2. **Workflow coordinator** — Structures RFQ, quotation, negotiation, deal, and milestone workflows
3. **Document manager** — Stores, tracks, and routes trade documents
4. **Verification coordinator** — Records verification statuses and documents
5. **Compliance information system** — Provides corridor-specific compliance checklists based on documented rules
6. **Transaction record system** — Records payment milestones, inspection results, and deal progress
7. **Communication platform** — Facilitates messaging between trade parties

---

## What AATOS Is Not

AATOS explicitly does **not** act as:

| Role | Why Not | Who Does |
|---|---|---|
| **Buyer** | AATOS does not purchase goods | The buyer organization on the platform |
| **Seller** | AATOS does not sell goods | The supplier organization on the platform |
| **Importer of record** | AATOS is not a legal entity in the destination country | The buyer or their appointed customs broker |
| **Exporter of record** | AATOS is not a legal entity in the origin country | The supplier or their appointed agent |
| **Customs broker** | AATOS does not file customs declarations | Licensed customs brokers |
| **Freight forwarder** | AATOS does not arrange shipping | Licensed freight forwarders |
| **Carrier** | AATOS does not transport goods | Shipping lines, airlines, trucking companies |
| **Inspection company** | AATOS does not perform physical inspections | Licensed inspection firms (SGS, Bureau Veritas, etc.) |
| **Laboratory** | AATOS does not test product quality | Accredited laboratories |
| **Certification authority** | AATOS does not issue phytosanitary, organic, or quality certificates | Government agencies and accredited certifiers |
| **Bank** | AATOS does not hold banking licenses | Licensed financial institutions |
| **Lender** | AATOS does not provide credit | Banks and trade finance providers |
| **Insurer** | AATOS does not underwrite risk | Licensed insurance companies |
| **Escrow company** | AATOS does not custody customer funds | Licensed payment providers and escrow services |
| **Legal advisor** | AATOS does not provide legal advice | Qualified attorneys in relevant jurisdictions |
| **Tax advisor** | AATOS does not provide tax advice | Qualified tax professionals |
| **Guarantor** | AATOS does not guarantee transactions, product quality, or payment | No third party guarantees transactions |

## Platform Liability Boundary

AATOS liability is limited to:

- **Platform availability** — Reasonable uptime of the software service
- **Data integrity** — Accurate recording of user-submitted information
- **Security** — Reasonable protection of user data

AATOS is **not liable** for:

- Product quality or fitness for purpose
- Regulatory compliance of any transaction
- Payment default by buyer or supplier
- Shipping delays or cargo damage
- Accuracy of user-submitted information
- Actions or omissions of third-party service providers
- Force majeure events

### Context
The platform must have clear legal boundaries before any real transaction occurs. Ambiguity creates unlimited liability.

### Decision
AATOS is exclusively a technology platform and marketplace operator. All regulated activities (banking, insurance, customs, inspection, certification) remain with licensed third parties.

### Consequences
- Limits liability to software service failures
- Requires clear terms of service
- Requires user acknowledgment of platform boundaries
- Prevents regulatory overreach into licensed activities

### Dependencies
- Unblocks: Terms of Service drafting, liability cap calculation
- Requires: Legal counsel review before publication

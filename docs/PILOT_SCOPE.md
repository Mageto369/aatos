## Exclusions

The following are explicitly excluded from the pilot:

- Roasted coffee
- Instant coffee
- Coffee extracts
- Flavored coffee
- Retail-ready consumer products
- Unverified organic claims
- Unverified sustainability claims

## Platform Boundaries

### AATOS Provides

- Supplier and buyer verification
- Structured product information for green coffee
- RFQs, quotations, negotiation records
- Deals, contracts
- Compliance checklists
- Document management
- Inspection coordination
- Payment milestone tracking
- Audit records

### AATOS Does NOT Provide

- Importer of record services
- Government certifications
- Legal approval
- Customer fund custody
- Product quality guarantees
- Transaction completion guarantees

### Responsibilities

- **Seller:** Accurate product and export information
- **Buyer or appointed representative:** Importer-of-record obligations
- **Licensed providers:** Regulated payments, inspections, laboratories, customs brokerage

# 2026-08-09 — Strategic Pivot Session

## Pilot Readiness: APPROVED

The user approved pilot readiness with explicit constraints:
- Kenya → U.S. green coffee only
- 20 orgs max
- Sandbox payments
- Manual compliance oversight

This is a deliberate risk-constrained launch model.

## The Strategic Shift

From: "Can we build this?"
To: "Will real buyers and sellers trust AATOS enough to move meaningful agricultural transactions through it?"

## Phase 4A Defined

**Name:** Live Pilot and Revenue Validation
**Goal:** Prove AATOS produces successful trade, not more software.
**Duration:** 30-60 days

### Operating Targets
- 5 Kenyan suppliers
- 5 U.S. buyers
- 10 serious RFQs
- 20-30 quotations
- 3 accepted deals
- 1-3 completed commercial transactions

### Exit Gate Criteria
- At least one real commercial transaction completed
- At least three accepted deals
- At least ten qualified RFQs
- No critical authorization or compliance incident
- No unresolved payment reconciliation issue
- Full transaction audit trail
- Measured off-platform leakage
- Buyer and supplier feedback collected

## Reordered Technology Priorities

1. **Real payment integration** — Integrate licensed provider, keep milestone orchestration
2. **Email/transactional notifications** — Cannot depend on users checking dashboards
3. **Pilot analytics** — Internal operating dashboard, not BI product
4. **Document verification** — Human workflow first, AI-assisted later
5. **Search** — Keep PostgreSQL until volume proves otherwise
6. **Mobile** — Web-first excellence, delay React Native
7. **AI matching** — After 100 transactions teach what matching means
8. **Blockchain** — Removed from current roadmap

## Key Principle

Exit points (WhatsApp, email, Excel, contracts) become the real roadmap. Record every exit point.
